---
title: Kanel
description: A comparison of `no-orm` to Kanel.
---

[Kanel](https://github.com/kristiandupont/kanel) is awesome. In fact, `no-orm` uses Kanel's [database schema parser](https://github.com/kristiandupont/extract-pg-schema) to read database schemas and generate code.

`Kanel` reads your Postgres schema and generates Typescript types.

`no-orm` takes this idea a bit further and adds in Zod parsing on those types as well as `orm-like` access patterns.

Shout-out to `Kanel`.
